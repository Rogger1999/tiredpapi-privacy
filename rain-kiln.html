Rain Kiln privacy policy

Developer: Tired Papi. Last updated: 1 October 2026.

What the game does

Rain Kiln runs offline. It does not collect or share personal data, connect to a server, display advertisements, or require an account. It contains no advertising or analytics SDKs.

Saved progress

The game stores your campaign progress, score and upgrades locally on your device. These records are not sent to Tired Papi or another service. They remain on the device until you clear the app's storage or uninstall it, which removes your saved progress. Rain Kiln does not provide an online account or cloud save service.

Purchases and platform services

Google Play processes the purchase separately. Rain Kiln does not receive your payment details. Google Play and your operating system may process information under their own privacy policies independently of the game.

Children

The same offline behavior applies to every player, including children. Rain Kiln has no account registration, chat, advertising, or personal-data collection.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact Tired Papi at tiredpapi@gmail.com. If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you send. We use it to answer your request and retain it only as needed for support and applicable obligations. You may request deletion by emailing us.
