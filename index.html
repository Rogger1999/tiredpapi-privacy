Crazy Shooting Style — Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 24, 2026. Developer: Tired Papi.

Crazy Shooting Style is an offline action game. The game does not collect or transmit personal information to Tired Papi. It does not include advertising, analytics, tracking SDKs, user accounts, chat, or in-app purchases.

Data on your device

Game progress and preferences, including sound settings and character selection, are saved locally on your device. They are not sent to Tired Papi. You can remove them by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it. Device backup services may retain a copy according to your device settings.

Store services

Google Play or Apple's App Store processes purchases and may collect store, diagnostic, and device information under its own privacy policy. Tired Papi does not receive your payment card details through the game.

Support

If you email tiredpapi@gmail.com, we receive your email address and whatever information you choose to send. We use this information to answer your request and keep it only as needed for support and applicable obligations. You may request deletion by emailing us.

Audience

The game contains stylized violence, blood and dismemberment and is not directed at children.

Changes and contact

We will update this page if the game's data practices change. For privacy questions contact Tired Papi at tiredpapi@gmail.com.
